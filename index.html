Medical Care for Stray & Wild Animals

Pravya Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to rescuing, treating, and rehabilitating stray and wild animals in need of urgent medical care.

About Us

Pravya Foundation works with veterinarians, volunteers, and wildlife experts to ensure injured and abandoned animals receive timely and compassionate medical treatment. Our mission is to protect animal life and promote coexistence between humans and animals.

Our Work

Emergency Rescue

Rapid response for injured stray animals and distressed wildlife in urban and forest areas.

Medical Treatment

On-site first aid, surgeries, vaccinations, and long-term medical care through partner veterinary clinics.

Rehabilitation

Safe recovery spaces and monitored rehabilitation before animals are released back into their natural habitat.

Awareness & Education

Community programs focused on animal welfare, wildlife safety, and responsible coexistence.

Your support can save lives. Join us as a volunteer, partner with us, or contribute to our medical relief efforts.
